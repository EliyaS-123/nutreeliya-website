Here are two proven, gluten-free quinoa Buddha bowl recipes you can make at home:

1) Gluten-Free Asian Quinoa Buddha Bowl
Serves 2
- Ingredients:
  - 1 bag (10 oz) Path of Life Asian Wok Quinoa Blend, cooked
  - Mushrooms, sliced
  - 1 shallot, minced
  - Gluten-free soy sauce (tamari)
  - 1/2 cup shredded red cabbage
  - 1/2 cup bean sprouts (washed and dried)
  - Avocado, sliced
  - Firm tofu, cubed
  - Oil for sautéing
- Directions:
  1) Cook the quinoa blend according to package directions.
  2) Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high. Add mushrooms; cook 4–5 minutes until golden. Add shallot; cook 30 seconds. Remove from heat and stir in tamari.
  3) Add cooked quinoa to bowls. Arrange mushrooms, cabbage, bean sprouts, avocado, and tofu on top. Serve immediately. [Source 1]

2) Quinoa–Chickpea Buddha Bowl with Tahini (Vegan)
- Ingredients:
  - Cooked quinoa
  - Chickpeas (cooked from scratch or canned/rinsed)
  - Kale (steamed or massaged)
  - Tahini sauce (simple lemon-tahini sauce)
  - Optional: roasted sweet potatoes and avocado
- Directions:
  1) Prepare chickpeas (cook from scratch or use canned and rinsed). Cook quinoa.
  2) Steam or massage kale.
  3) Make a simple tahini sauce. 
  4) Assemble: Divide quinoa, chickpeas, and kale into bowls and drizzle with tahini sauce. Add optional roasted sweet potatoes and avocado for extra flavor and texture. [Source 2][Source 5]

Notes:
- Quinoa is naturally gluten-free; use tamari or gluten-free soy sauce in any sauces to keep the bowls celiac-safe. [Source 1][Source 2]
