Smart gluten-free cooking tips
- Focus on naturally gluten-free staples first (quinoa, rice, potatoes, legumes, vegetables, fruits, meats, fish, eggs, nuts). They’re inherently safe and nutrient-dense [Source 6].
- Prevent cross-contamination: Use separate toasters, cutting boards, and colanders; even traces can cause issues for people with celiac disease [Source 6].
- Read every label and look for “certified gluten-free,” especially for broths, spice blends, and sauces. For soy sauce, use tamari or coconut aminos; for thickeners, use cornstarch or potato starch in place of wheat flour [Source 6].
- Round out meals with GF whole grains (e.g., quinoa) and simple salads to keep dinners balanced throughout the week [Source 5].
