Here’s a proven gluten-free quinoa Buddha bowl recipe with clear steps and celiac-safe tips.

Gluten-Free Quinoa Buddha Bowl with Lemon-Tahini Dressing
- Time: 25 minutes (Prep 10, Cook 15). Serves: 2. Nutrition per serving: ~320 cal, 14g protein, 42g carbs, 12g fat, 8g fiber [Source 6].

Ingredients
- 1 cup quinoa, rinsed
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 2 cups baby spinach
- 1 medium sweet potato, diced (~200g)
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/2 avocado, sliced
- Dressing: 2 tbsp tahini, juice of 1 lemon, 1 clove garlic (minced), 2 tbsp warm water, salt & pepper to taste [Source 6]

Instructions
1) Cook quinoa: Add rinsed quinoa to 2 cups water, bring to a boil, reduce heat, cover, and simmer ~15 minutes until fluffy [Source 6].
2) Roast sweet potato: Toss diced sweet potato with olive oil, salt, and pepper; roast at 400°F (200°C) for ~20 minutes until golden and tender [Source 6].
3) Make dressing: Whisk tahini, lemon juice, minced garlic, and warm water until smooth; season with salt and pepper [Source 6].
4) Assemble bowls: Base of quinoa, then baby spinach, roasted sweet potato, chickpeas, and avocado. Drizzle with lemon-tahini dressing [Source 6].

Gluten-free/celiac safety
- Naturally gluten-free ingredients; verify tahini is from a certified GF facility (Soom and Bob’s Red Mill offer GF options). Store components separately up to 4 days for meal prep [Source 6].

Optional variations (still gluten-free)
- Use kale instead of spinach and steam it before assembling, as in similar quinoa-chickpea bowls with tahini sauce [Source 1].
- Roast the chickpeas for added crunch and include kale alongside sweet potatoes and avocado, then finish with tahini dressing [Source 7].
